# Framework Agreement — Technical Facility Maintenance — C-2024-003

## §1 Introduction

This framework agreement governs technical maintenance of the Municipality's
property portfolio and is entered into between Sundhamn Municipality and
Vasaberg Teknik AB, org. no. 556433-7719. The Agreement is jointly administered
by the Facilities Management division and the Property Management unit.

## §2 Duration

The Agreement runs from 15 January 2024 to 14 January 2026 with an option for
the Municipality to extend by up to twenty-four (24) months.

## §3 Scope

The Supplier undertakes preventive and corrective maintenance of ventilation,
heating, and electrical installations in the properties listed in Appendix 1,
including statutory ventilation inspections (OVK) and periodic electrical
safety checks.

## §4 Rates

### §4.1 Hourly rates by personnel category

Work performed under this Agreement shall be compensated according to the
following rates, excluding VAT:

| Personnel category   | Description                                        | Rate (SEK/hour) |
|----------------------|----------------------------------------------------|-----------------|
| Technician           | Routine preventive and corrective maintenance      | 745             |
| Senior technician    | Fault diagnosis, commissioning, statutory checks   | 895             |
| Emergency call-out   | Attendance outside 07:00–17:00 on weekdays         | 1 340           |

The personnel category applied shall be stated for each line on the invoice.
Where an assignment is performed by a senior technician but the work falls
within the routine maintenance description, the technician rate shall apply.

### §4.2 Materials

Materials and spare parts are invoiced at documented cost plus a handling
surcharge of five (5) per cent. Material costs shall be itemised separately.

## §5 Volume limits

### §5.1 Monthly ceiling

The aggregate number of hours invoiced under §4.1 shall not exceed 120 hours
per calendar month unless the Property Management unit has approved the excess
in writing in advance.

## §6 Response times

Emergency faults affecting operations shall be attended to within four (4)
hours of notification. Non-urgent faults shall be attended to within five (5)
working days.

## §7 Invoicing

Invoices shall state the contract number, the property designation, the
personnel category, the number of hours, and the applicable rate.
