# Agreement on Professional Development Services — C-2024-006

## §1 Parties

Sundhamn Municipality, Education administration, and Almgren Utbildning HB,
org. no. 969776-1120. The Agreement was signed in paper form and is archived as
a scanned document.

## §2 Period

The Agreement is valid from 1 August 2024 to 31 July 2026.

## §3 Subject matter

### §3.1 Training covered

The Supplier shall deliver in-service professional development for teaching
staff in the following areas: pedagogical leadership in the classroom;
assessment and grading practice; and the use of digital tools in teaching. Each
training day comprises a minimum of six contact hours including preparation and
follow-up documentation.

### §3.2 Training not covered

Coaching of individual employees, organisational development consultancy, and
recruitment services are not covered by this Agreement.

## §4 Fees

### §4.1 Fee per training day

The Municipality shall pay SEK 12 500 per training day excluding VAT. The fee
covers the trainer's time, course materials for up to twenty-five (25)
participants, and travel within the municipality.

### §4.2 Additional participants

Where more than twenty-five participants attend, an additional SEK 350 per
participant shall be paid to cover materials.

## §5 Volume

### §5.1 Annual ceiling

A maximum of 20 training days per contract year may be invoiced under this
Agreement.

## §6 Cancellation

Cancellation later than ten (10) working days before a booked training day
entitles the Supplier to fifty (50) per cent of the fee.

## §7 Invoicing

Invoices shall state the contract number, the training area, the date of
delivery, and the number of participants.
