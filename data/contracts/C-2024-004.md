# Contract for Drainage and Groundworks — C-2024-004

## §1 The parties

Sundhamn Municipality, Urban Development administration, and Lindqvist
Anläggning AB, org. no. 556288-9014.

## §2 Contract period

1 April 2024 to 30 September 2025.

## §3 Works to be performed

The Contractor shall lay stormwater drainage along the routes identified in the
tender documents, including excavation, bedding, laying of pipe, backfilling,
and reinstatement of the surface to its previous condition.

## §4 Remuneration

### §4.1 Unit price for drainage

The Works shall be remunerated on a unit-price basis at SEK 640 per running
metre of completed drainage excluding VAT. The unit price is inclusive of
excavation, materials, labour, plant, and reinstatement, and no separate charge
may be made for these elements.

### §4.2 Price adjustment

The unit price is fixed for the duration of the contract period and is not
subject to indexation.

## §5 Volume

### §5.1 Maximum length

The total length remunerated under this Contract shall not exceed 500 running
metres over the contract period.

## §6 Completion and inspection

Completed sections shall be inspected jointly before backfilling. The
Contractor shall give at least two working days' notice of readiness for
inspection.

## §7 Invoicing

Invoices shall be submitted on completion of each inspected section and shall
state the contract number, the section designation, and the number of running
metres completed.
