# Agreement on School Meal Provision — C-2024-007

## §1 Parties

Sundhamn Municipality, Education administration, in cooperation with the school
catering unit, and Kvarnholmen Livsmedel AB, org. no. 556140-6677.

## §2 Term

1 January 2024 to 31 December 2025.

## §3 Deliverables and price

### §3.1 Price per portion

The Supplier shall deliver prepared school lunch portions to the units listed
in Appendix 1. The Municipality shall pay SEK 68.50 per delivered portion
excluding VAT. The price includes preparation, packaging, chilled transport,
and delivery to the receiving kitchen.

### §3.2 Special diets

Portions prepared to meet documented medical or religious dietary requirements
are delivered at the same price per portion. No surcharge may be applied for
special diets.

### §3.3 Nutritional requirements

Meals shall meet the National Food Agency's recommendations for school meals.
The Supplier shall provide nutritional documentation on request.

## §4 Volume

### §4.1 Weekly ceiling

The Supplier may deliver and invoice a maximum of 4 000 portions per week.
Volumes above this level require a written amendment agreed with the school
catering unit.

## §5 Delivery

Deliveries shall arrive between 09:00 and 10:30 on school days. Portions
delivered outside the agreed temperature range shall be replaced at the
Supplier's cost and shall not be invoiced.

## §6 Invoicing

Invoices shall be submitted monthly and shall state the contract number, the
receiving unit, the number of portions delivered per week, and the price per
portion.
