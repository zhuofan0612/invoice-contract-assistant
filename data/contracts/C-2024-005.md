# Agreement on Managed Workplace Operations — C-2024-005

## §1 Parties and purpose

This Agreement between Sundhamn Municipality and Sundberg IT-Drift AB, org. no.
556077-3382, governs the operation, monitoring, and support of the
Municipality's managed client devices. The Agreement is administered by the IT
department.

## §2 Term

The Agreement applies from 1 February 2024 to 31 January 2027.

## §3 Services included

The monthly fee under §4.1 covers device enrolment, patch management, endpoint
monitoring, anti-malware operation, backup of user profile data, and first- and
second-line support during office hours. The fee is charged per device
registered in the management platform on the last calendar day of the month.

## §4 Pricing

### §4.1 Tiered price per device

The monthly fee per managed device is determined by the total number of
registered devices according to the following tiers, excluding VAT:

| Tier | Number of registered devices | Fee per device and month (SEK) |
|------|------------------------------|--------------------------------|
| 1    | 1–50                         | 210                            |
| 2    | 51–200                       | 175                            |
| 3    | 201 and above                | 140                            |

The tier is determined by the total device count for the month in question and
is applied to all devices in that month. Tiers are not applied cumulatively.

### §4.2 Support outside office hours

Support requested outside 07:00–18:00 on weekdays is charged at SEK 1 090 per
commenced hour and shall be pre-approved by the IT department.

## §5 Volume limits

### §5.1 Device ceiling

The number of devices registered under this Agreement shall not exceed 400
without a written amendment to the Agreement.

## §6 Service levels

Availability of the management platform shall be at least 99.5 per cent
measured monthly. Failure to meet the service level entitles the Municipality
to a price reduction of ten (10) per cent of the monthly fee.

## §7 Invoicing

Invoices shall be issued monthly in arrears stating the contract number, the
number of registered devices, the tier applied, and the fee per device.
