# Agreement on Cleaning Services for School Premises — C-2024-002

## §1 Contracting parties

Sundhamn Municipality, Facilities Management division ("the Municipality"), and
Björkdal Städservice AB, org. no. 556901-2231 ("the Supplier"). The Agreement
covers the school premises listed in Appendix 1.

## §2 Period of validity

The Agreement applies from 1 March 2024 until 28 February 2026.

## §3 Services and unit prices

### §3.1 Routine cleaning

Routine cleaning of classrooms, corridors, and sanitary facilities shall be
performed according to the cleaning specification in Appendix 2. The
Municipality shall pay SEK 485 per cleaning occasion excluding VAT. One
cleaning occasion comprises the full scope set out in the specification for one
school unit.

### §3.2 Window cleaning

Periodic window cleaning, including interior and exterior surfaces up to a
height of four metres, shall be performed on call-off. The Municipality shall
pay SEK 1 250 per occasion excluding VAT.

### §3.3 Services not covered

The Agreement does not cover outdoor or seasonal grounds services. In
particular, snow clearance, gritting, lawn care, leaf removal, and clearing of
external car parks and walkways fall outside the scope of this Agreement and
shall not be invoiced hereunder. Such services are procured under the
Municipality's grounds maintenance agreement.

## §4 Quality and follow-up

The Supplier shall carry out a documented quality inspection each quarter. Any
deficiency reported by the Municipality shall be remedied within two working
days at no additional cost.

## §5 Volume limits

### §5.1 Maximum number of occasions

The Supplier may invoice a maximum of 22 cleaning occasions per calendar month.
Additional occasions require a written call-off from the Facilities Management
division and shall be referenced on the invoice.

## §6 Invoicing

Invoices shall be submitted monthly and shall state the contract number, the
school unit, the number of occasions, and the unit price applied.
