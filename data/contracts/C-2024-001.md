# Framework Agreement for IT Advisory and Integration Services — C-2024-001

## §1 Parties

This framework agreement ("the Agreement") is entered into between Sundhamn
Municipality, org. no. 212000-1355 ("the Municipality"), and Nordisk Konsult AB,
org. no. 556712-4408 ("the Supplier"). The Agreement is administered by the
Municipality's IT department.

## §2 Term

The Agreement enters into force on 1 January 2024 and remains in force until
31 December 2025. The Municipality may extend the Agreement by twelve (12)
months on unchanged terms by giving written notice no later than three months
before expiry.

## §3 Scope of services

### §3.1 Included services

The Supplier shall, upon call-off, provide the following services: strategic
advisory concerning the Municipality's IT architecture; review of system
architecture and technical documentation; integration work between the
Municipality's existing case management systems; configuration and technical
implementation of procured software; and technical project support in
connection with system replacement.

### §3.2 Excluded services

For the avoidance of doubt, the Agreement does not cover the supply of
hardware, licences, or third-party software. The Supplier shall not invoice the
Municipality for the purchase, lease, or resale of physical equipment such as
computers, servers, network equipment, or peripherals. Any such requirement
shall be procured separately under the Municipality's hardware framework
agreement.

## §4 Compensation

### §4.1 Strategic advisory

For strategic advisory services as described in §3.1, the Supplier shall be
compensated at a fixed hourly rate of SEK 1 450 per hour excluding VAT. This
rate applies to advisory assignments performed by a consultant at senior
adviser level and covers preparatory work, meetings, and written
recommendations.

### §4.2 Implementation and integration

For implementation, configuration, and integration work as described in §3.1,
the Supplier shall be compensated at a fixed hourly rate of SEK 1 150 per hour
excluding VAT. This rate applies irrespective of the seniority of the
individual consultant performing the work.

### §4.3 Expenses

Travel time is not compensated. Documented travel expenses are reimbursed at
cost against receipt and shall be itemised separately on the invoice.

## §5 Volume limits

### §5.1 Monthly ceiling

The total volume called off under this Agreement shall not exceed 160 hours per
calendar month without the prior written approval of the Municipality's IT
director. Hours exceeding this ceiling without such approval shall not be
compensated.

## §6 Invoicing

Invoices shall be issued monthly in arrears and shall state the contract
number, the assignment reference, the number of hours per rate category, and
the applicable hourly rate. Payment terms are thirty (30) days from a correct
invoice.
