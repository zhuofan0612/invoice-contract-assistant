# Agreement on Security Services — C-2024-008

## §1 Parties

Sundhamn Municipality, Security and Preparedness unit, and Trelleborg Säkerhet
AB, org. no. 556319-2204.

## §2 Term

The Agreement is valid from 1 May 2024 to 30 April 2026.

## §3 Scope of the assignment

The Supplier shall provide manned guarding of municipal premises, alarm
response, and security-related consultancy in accordance with call-offs from
the Security and Preparedness unit. Assignments are ordered per object and are
documented in a written call-off stating the object designation and the nature
of the assignment.

## §4 Remuneration

### §4.1 Manned guarding

For manned guarding, patrol, and alarm response, the Supplier shall be paid
SEK 520 per hour excluding VAT. This rate applies to the presence of a
qualified security officer at the object and includes reporting.

### §4.2 Security consultancy

For security consultancy, including risk and vulnerability analysis, protective
security review, and advice on physical protection, the Supplier shall be paid
SEK 1 180 per hour excluding VAT.

### §4.3 Classification of assignments

Where an assignment comprises elements of both §4.1 and §4.2, the written
call-off shall state the division of hours between the two rate categories. In
the absence of such a statement the Municipality is entitled to require the
Supplier to substantiate the classification before payment.

## §5 Volume limits

### §5.1 Monthly ceiling

The Supplier may invoice a maximum of 300 hours per calendar month in
aggregate across §4.1 and §4.2.

## §6 Personnel requirements

All personnel deployed shall hold a valid guard authorisation and shall be
approved by the Municipality before deployment.

## §7 Invoicing

Invoices shall state the contract number, the object designation, the rate
category under §4.1 or §4.2, the number of hours, and the applicable rate.
